Frequently asked questions

Know exactly what you're getting

Is QuotePilot a monthly subscription?

No. The current downloadable quote builder costs $9 once and has no recurring subscription fee.

Do I need to install special software?

No. Download and unzip the product, then open its HTML application in a compatible desktop web browser.

Can I send quotes directly from QuotePilot?

QuotePilot does not automatically email quotes. Save your estimate as a PDF using your browser, then share that PDF using your preferred email or messaging tool.

Does QuotePilot work offline?

Yes. The downloaded browser-based application is designed to work offline after you open the file.

Does QuotePilot handle payments or accounting?

No. This version creates service estimates; it does not process payments, provide cloud synchronization, or perform bookkeeping or tax filing.

How do I keep my quote data safe?

Use the built-in backup feature and keep copies of important estimates. QuotePilot is a local browser-based tool, not a cloud backup service.
