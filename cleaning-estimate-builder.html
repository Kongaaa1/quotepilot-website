Practical quoting guide

What should a cleaning estimate include?

A useful cleaning quote tells the customer what work is included, what it costs, and what to expect before the appointment.

1. Scope of work

List the type of cleaning, property size or rooms covered, and any extras such as appliances, windows, or deep cleaning.

2. Clear line-item prices

Separate your base service from optional add-ons. Note any discounts, taxes where applicable, and the total.

3. Terms and validity

Include customer details, a quote date, expiration date, and any deposit or payment expectations.

Is an estimate the same as an invoice?

No. An estimate communicates expected pricing before the work; an invoice requests payment for work performed or agreed charges. QuotePilot's current product focuses on estimates, not invoicing.
